About SMART PARK

Learn about our mission and what drives us to innovate parking solutions.

Our Story

SMART PARK was founded in 2022 by three passionate entrepreneurs: Zain Abbas, Ahmad Yahya, and Murtaza Sarwar. After experiencing firsthand the frustration of finding parking in busy urban areas of Gujrat, Pakistan, they created a solution using technology.

The founders have equally contributed their expertise and dedication. Today, SMART PARK offers a comprehensive system to help drivers find, reserve, and pay for parking effortlessly.

Our Mission

At SMART PARK, our mission is to transform the way people park their vehicles by providing innovative, user-friendly solutions that save time, reduce stress, and contribute to smarter urban mobility in Pakistan and beyond.

We believe that parking shouldn't be a hassle. By leveraging technology and data, we aim to create more efficient parking systems that benefit both drivers and parking facility operators, starting from our home in Gujrat.
