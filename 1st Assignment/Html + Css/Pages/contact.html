Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reserve a parking spot?

To reserve a parking spot, create an account, search for available spots in your desired location, select a spot, and complete the payment process.

Can I cancel my reservation?

Yes, you can cancel your reservation up to 2 hours before your scheduled parking time for a full refund.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and mobile payment solutions like Apple Pay and Google Pay.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your payment information.
